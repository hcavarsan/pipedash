- Logs and status of running jobs update live, with a Live marker and Jump to latest when you scroll up. GitHub logs still arrive when the job ends.
- Running runs show their elapsed time and how long they usually take.
- ArgoCD, FluxCD and Komodo pipelines show what their live resources are doing, and workloads with failing pods show as Degraded.
- Lists follow iOS patterns: two-line rows, repositories as sections, and a More menu in the toolbar for Select, Filter, Sort by and Group by.
- Failed GitLab, Gitea, Bitbucket, Tekton and Jenkins logs point at their error lines instead of saying No errors.
- With Lock when leaving the app on, the previous screen no longer shows before the lock screen when you come back.
- A canceling GitLab pipeline shows as running, and Komodo shows the last run of quiet stacks again.
- Pipedash sends a failed trigger, rerun or cancel only once, and view rules stay editable after you remove one of their providers.
