# Support

Need help with Pipedash on iOS, macOS, Linux, Windows or the web server?

- Email: [hcavarsan@yahoo.com.br](mailto:hcavarsan@yahoo.com.br). No GitHub account needed.
- Bug reports and feature requests: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hcavarsan/pipedash/issues).

Include your Pipedash version (Settings > About), your device and the provider you use.
Do not send provider tokens, passwords or device tokens by email or in a public issue.

See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for how Pipedash handles your data.
